## 0.3.0 (2026-10-23)

A packaging release; no algorithmic change. The configuration `c46` (config hash <Claim id="freeze.config_hash" />) is bit-identical to the frozen one.

- **Breaking: the import name is now `amm_optimizer`.** 0.2.0 installed the top-level modules `atm` and `amm`, which collide with unrelated
  PyPI projects of those names; both modules are gone. `ATMConfig` and the preset names (`c46`) are unchanged.
- **The default configuration is unchanged.** `amm_optimizer.minimize` without `config=` runs <Claim id="pkg.default_config" />. AMM needs `config=amm_optimizer.presets.get("c46")`.
- The package requires <Claim id="pkg.requires" />. 0.2.0 declared lower floors that SciPy 1.16 or newer cannot satisfy.
- Fixed: `import` no longer fails on NumPy 1.x; the `c46` preset no longer crashes for one-dimensional problems at budgets of 150 or more.
- Added: `py.typed`, an installed-package smoke test, GitHub Actions workflows (CI, release, security checks), `SECURITY.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## 0.2.0 (release of the freeze)

Packaging of the frozen code: import as `amm` (the implementation kept its code name `atm`), `CITATION.cff` and an AMM README. No algorithmic
change. It was published to PyPI and has since been yanked: its default `minimize` is the original ATM sampler and it imports as `amm` or `atm`. 0.3.0 replaces it.

## Development history before 0.2.0

The entries below describe the code as it was at the time, when the module was `atm`.

### 2026-10-22: code freeze

- The configuration `c46` is frozen for the paper (`docs/FREEZE_2026-10-22.md`,
  config hash <Claim id="freeze.config_hash" />). The algorithm does not change for the final test.
- Cycle 71: an opt-in option `stage_extend_max_per_dim` (the `stage_extend_restart` option only up to a
  budget per dimension). The capped version was not confirmed on fresh ids, so it is **not** in any preset.
- Batch 91 was rejected under the letter of its pre-set rule. `c46` is unchanged.

### 2026-10-22: cycle 70, `c46`

- New preset `c46` (`c45` plus `prefix_low_beta=0.15` from dimension 10): in the evolution-strategy prefix at
  100 x D, a finite rugged curvature reading below the threshold keeps the (1+1)-CMA-ES instead of the lq2
  populations (aimed at bbob f23). Confirmed on fresh ids and promoted. It is the best composite.
- Opt-in options added and not in a preset: `centre_point` / `centre_point_min_dim`, `smooth_bfgs_second`.
- Batch 89 (`stage_extend_restart` on `c45`) was rejected.

### 2026-10-22: cycles 67-69, `c45`

- New preset `c45`. It adds a rugged-route BFGS try that continues to the end of the budget on a kink
  reading, a ridge reading that hands the rest of the prefix to restarted COBYQA, retries of a descent that
  did not move, a band where the finisher's populations start at once, and `box_normalize`. Confirmed on
  fresh ids.
- `Variant.box_normalize`: `minimize` maps any box onto [-5, 5] in each coordinate and runs there. It is
  bit-identical on bbob, where the box is already [-5, 5].
- Several other options were added as opt-in and left out of the presets, or rejected, among them
  `sampled_polish_per_dim` and `sep_route_low`.

### 2026-10-21: cycles 63-66, `c44`

- New preset `c44`: plateau populations in the prefix from dimension 5, and `small_switch`, which reads the
  first SLSQP descent at two checkpoints at budgets up to 10 x D and may hand the rest to restarted COBYQA.
- `Variant.prefix_nan_reading`: where an undefined curvature reading of the prefix goes. Opt-in.

### 2026-10-21: cycles 52-61, presets `c39` to `c43`

- `c43`: `atm.cobyqa_phase.run_cobyqa_restarts` (restarted SciPy COBYQA in the box) and the small-engine
  option for the smallest budgets, in dimensions up to 3.
- `c42`: after `basin_route`'s smooth, single-basin reading at dimensions 2 and 3, SLSQP descents from the
  design, then the usual path.
- `c41`: `qn_phase.run_scaled_bfgs(box_qp=...)`, SLSQP's bound handling in a BFGS descent, and the route
  `qn44`.
- `c40`: a one-descent prefix (one SLSQP descent, then the (1+1)-ES) at small budgets.
- `c39`: `atm.basin_phase`: a Sobol design and a curvature probe send smooth multi-basin readings
  (Gallagher-type functions) at dimensions 2 and 3 to MLSL; a stall check on the separable search.
- `Variant.fork_seed` (research): independent continuations from one hand-over, for a router dataset.

### 2026-10-20: cycles 35-51, presets `c35` to `c38`

- `c38`: `atm.chain_phase` and the options `chain_route` and `sep_route`. A pair test of additivity sends a
  linear chain (bbob f20) to a decoupled coordinate search, and a separable function to restarted STEP.
- `c37`: a restarted nonsmooth BFGS on forward differences (`qn_phase.run_scaled_bfgs`) for the f13
  kink route and a capped try at dimensions 5 to 9.
- `c36`: a fractal-reading route to restarted SLSQP, and prefix switches (separable smooth prefix reaches
  SLSQP).
- `c35`: Gallagher-style restarts of SLSQP from the best unclaimed sample point.
- `step_phase.run_step_phase` keeps its samples in arrays: bit-exact and much faster at large budgets.

### 2026-10-19: integration of the research line, presets `c23` to `c34`

- The research line (composites `c23` to `c31`), the code-review branch and `refactor` were merged. Every
  characterised run of the research tip reproduces bit for bit, except 14 round-1 arms that use a removed
  option (`docs/MERGE_2026-10-19.md`).
- `atm.presets` holds the named configurations, with `get`, `names` and `with_changes`. Each preset equals
  `benchmarks/arms/<name>_config.txt`, and `quick_scan --config preset:NAME` reads them.
- Renames: `Variant.nm_max_dim` of `refactor` became `simplex_max_dim`; the research line's
  `ATMConfig.improved()` became `ATMConfig.improved_wave5()`, and `improved()` is the Tier 5 hybrid.
- `c32` to `c34` added the L-BFGS-B fallback when an SLSQP call fails more often than it converges, an
  IPOP route from dimension 20, a stall monitor on the first SLSQP descent, and a second descent from a
  uniform point.
- **Harness fix:** the cache of bbob optimal values (`f_opt`) held wrong values for 291 f12 instances
  because of rounding in a COCO data line. The harness now reads the exact value, repaired the cache,
  and flags runs that stopped on a false final-target hit. Every f12 score before this date is affected.
- `scipy>=1.16` became a declared dependency.
- Research tooling added: a posterior goal ledger, fresh-id claim scripts and a consolidated PDF builder.

### Before the research line (undated entries)

The repository changelog does not date these entries.

- `ATMConfig.variant` (`atm.config.Variant`): opt-in algorithm variants, every default equal to the original
  and tested bit for bit; `ATMConfig.to_dict`, `from_dict`, `config_hash`; `minimize(bounds=...)`.
- `ATMConfig.improved()`: the best configuration of `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md` (needs `bounds=`);
  `ATMConfig()` stays the original. Later waves added a (1+1)-ES prefix, a learned covariance, a late
  Nelder-Mead refinement, and a wave-7 restriction of the local stages after a holdout found a regression
  on multimodal functions. `improved_wave6()` keeps the previous preset for reproducibility.
- A scoring fix (exact bbob optima instead of cached values that floored converged runs), raw COCO
  evaluation and the 2019 start protocol; `benchmarks/quick_scan.py` version 2 (stable seeds,
  provenance, process pool, resume, cluster bootstrap).
- The COCO/bbob harness (`benchmarks/run_bbob.py` and companions, `docs/BENCHMARKS.md`), block profiling
  (`atm.profiling`), a CI workflow, a `py.typed` marker, and faster parent selection (bit-identical).
- Opt-in variants that were added and then removed again for no aggregate gain are recorded in
  `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.

### 0.2.0.dev0 (development version)

- A clean re-implementation of ATM as the `atm` package, bit-identical to `BjBodner/atm@69828c6` with
  `ATMConfig(strict_budget=False)`.
- Strict budget by default (at most `budget` calls, all counted), handling of NaN and infinite objective
  values, conductor robustness, and an opt-in correct second best. All differences from the original are
  listed in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`.
