# Preset history

How the default configuration came about. Each research composite `c23` to `c46` is the one before it with the changes confirmed in one research cycle, measured on COCO/bbob instances not used to develop it (the "fresh ids"; the gains are in the share of targets reached). `c46` is the configuration frozen for the paper on 2026-10-22 and the default of `amm_optimizer.minimize` (`presets.get("default")`); [FREEZE_2026-10-22.md](FREEZE_2026-10-22.md) records what was frozen.

All of them stay loadable, so earlier results reproduce: `presets.get("c26")` returns the configuration of `c26`, and `presets.names(research=True)` lists every name. Each one equals the configuration text its snapshots in `tests/characterisation.json` were recorded with (`tests/test_presets.py` checks the configurations and their `config_hash`). Every preset needs `minimize(..., bounds=...)` and a deterministic objective; `amm_optimizer/api.py`'s module docstring has the order of the phases.

- `c23`: ATM's global phase with success-rate widths, a learned covariance and reflection into the box, as a (1+1)-CMA-ES prefix up to 100 x dim (Nelder-Mead at D <= 2); above that ATM for 20% of the budget (at most 60 dim), the separable STEP search, and the restarted (1+1)-Cholesky-CMA-ES with quadratic model steps, handing stalls (and runs past 150 dim, from D 10) to active population CMA-ES with twice the default lambda.
- `c24`: `c23` with the lq2-surrogate population prefix at 100 x dim on non-separable functions and a curvature probe in the hand-over's first run.
- `c25`: `c24` with the curvature-gated prefix: SLSQP where the probe reads the function as smooth, lq2 populations where it reads rugged (from 30 x dim).
- `c26`: `c25` with an SLSQP stage (`cma_local_nonsep="qn3"`) before the CMA finisher on smooth non-separable functions.
- `c27`: `c26` with the gated prefix also at 10 x dim from 200 evaluations (`slsqplq10c`), one SLSQP descent before Nelder-Mead at D 2 up to 30 x dim (`qnprefix30`) and the curvature-routed local phase `qn7` (confirmed on fresh ids 136-145).
- `c28`: `c27` with the plateau route `qn8` (IPOP populations with 4x lambda on plateaus such as f7; confirmed on ids 146-155).
- `c29`: `c28` with SLSQP's finite-difference step 1e-9 in the prefix (`slsqplq10h`) and the local stage (`qn9`).
- `c30`: `c29` with the local phase back to `qn8` (`qn9` was rejected for Gallagher; the prefix's 1e-9 step stays).
- `c31`: `c30` with `qn12` (the 1e-9 step in the stage, SciPy's in the Gallagher restarts); confirmed on fresh ids 166-175.
- `c32`: `c31` with `qn_fallback="lbfgsb"`: an SLSQP call whose failed descents outnumber its converged ones hands the rest to restarted L-BFGS-B, and a local stage that ends that way gives L-BFGS-B the rest of the budget (f12, bent cigar); confirmed on fresh ids 176-185 (f12 +.296; +.208 over pycma-IPOP).
- `c33`: `c32` with the local route `qn17`: from D 20, a rugged reading with at least 640 x D left runs unprotected IPOP populations from a uniform point (4x the default lambda, 8x with at least 2500 x D left, doubling per restart); confirmed on fresh ids 186-195 (f17-f18 at D 20-40 +.171).
- `c34`: `c33` with the local route `qn19`: when the stage's first SLSQP descent is still running at its cap, a stall monitor read once decides whether it continues to SciPy's own exit, and a converged gated descent with at least 400 x D left gets one more descent from a uniform point (Rosenbrock's second minimum); confirmed on fresh ids 196-210 (f8 + f9 at D 20-40 +.172; every cell +.015).
- `c35`: `c34` with the local route `qn20`: Gallagher-like readings (a smooth stage with several basins) restart SLSQP from the best sample point not yet claimed by an earlier start or end, and stop descents that cannot catch the incumbent; confirmed on fresh ids 211-245 (f21 + f22 at D 10-40, 1000-3000 x D +.191, inside the `qn25` composite whose other path, the f13 kink route, did not confirm).
- `c36`: `c35` with the local route `qn29` (`fract2`: a fractal rugged reading, read by a probe moved off the bounds, leaves the rugged route for restarted SLSQP) and `lean30`'s prefix switches (a separable smooth prefix reaches SLSQP; no ATM tail from above 10 x D up to 100 x D); confirmed on fresh ids 246-285 (all functions at 30-100 x D +.016; f23 at D 20-40 +.044).
- `c37`: `c36` with the local route `qn38`: the f13 kink route from D 5 on a scaled-FD nonsmooth BFGS (`qn_phase.run_scaled_bfgs`), one capped BFGS descent on a rugged reading at D 5-9 up to 500 x D, and the rugged IPOP route from D 5 with 2000 x D left; confirmed on fresh ids 286-325 (f13 at D 10-40, 1000 x D +.304; f13 at D 5, 300 x D +.377; f15 at D 5-10, 3000 x D +.106).
- `c38`: `c37` with `chain_route` and `sep_route` (`chain_phase`): a pair test at x0 from D 5 sends a linear chain (bbob f20) to the decoupled coordinate search from 300 x D and a separable function to restarted STEP from 100 x D; confirmed on fresh ids 326-365 (f20 at 300-3000 x D +.773; f3 at 100 x D +.711).
- `c39`: `c38` with `basin_route` (`basin_phase`: at D 2-3 from 100 x D a Sobol design and a curvature probe send smooth multi-basin readings, Gallagher, to MLSL) and `sep_check="stall"` from 1000 x D (a stuck STEP leaves through a pair test); confirmed on fresh ids 490-529 (f21 + f22 at D 2-3 +.107; D 5, 3000 x D +.040).
- `c40`: `c39` with the one-descent prefix (`es_kind="slsqplq10hd"`: one SLSQP descent from the best point so far, then the (1+1)) below 31 x D and `QN_MIN_BUDGET` (`des_max_per_dim`), and the whole budget to the prefix at 10 x D (`prefix_full_min_per_dim=0`); confirmed on fresh ids 530-569 (D 5-10, 10 x D +.024; D 3-5, 30 x D +.050).
- `c41`: `c40` with route `qn44`: on a smooth reading up to 300 x D, one descent of the scaled-FD BFGS with SLSQP's bound handling (`box_qp`, the BFGS engine toy's f10 fix), then the usual staged route; confirmed on fresh ids 570-609 (f6 / f12 / f13 / f14 at D 5-40, 300 x D +.163; all functions +.033).
- `c42`: `c41` with `basin_descents=1` and `basin_keep_atm`: on `basin_route`'s smooth, not-multi-basin reading at D 2-3 up to 300 x D, one SLSQP descent from the design's best point, then the usual path from that point with ATM's full share; confirmed on the D 2 ids 400-449 (f6 / f8 / f9 / f12 / f13 at D 2-3, 100-300 x D +.103; all functions there +.016).
- `c43`: `c42` with `small_engine="cobyqa"` up to D 3: at budgets up to 10 x D, restarted SciPy COBYQA (`cobyqa_phase`, initial trust radius 0.25 of the box) spends the whole budget; confirmed on fresh ids 366-399 (D 2, 10 x D +.026; D 3 +.017).
- `c44`: `c43` with `prefix_plateau_pop` from D 5 (a plateau check in the ES prefix above 30 x D hands f7-like plateaus to restarted CMA-ES populations) and `small_switch` (at budgets up to 10 x D, D 4-20, the prefix's first SLSQP descent is read after one and two line searches and hands rugged or non-quadratic runs to restarted COBYQA); confirmed on fresh ids 610-649 (f7 at D 5-40, 100 x D +.079; all functions at D 5-20, 10 x D +.012).
- `c45`: `c44` with five confirmed changes: `rugged_try_rest="cutkink"` (the rugged route's capped BFGS try continues where its best point reads a kink, f13's ridge), the prefix's ridge reading (`prefix_ridge_after=7` up to 30 x D, which hands a descent that overshoots a sharp ridge to restarted COBYQA, with `prefix_nan_reading="plateau"`), `prefix_descent_retries=5` (the one-descent prefixes retry a descent that did not move), `rugged_direct_beta=1.0` / `rugged_direct_min_beta=0.5` (on that band of rugged hand-over readings the finisher's populations start at once), and `box_normalize` (any box is mapped onto [-5, 5]^D; bbob unchanged); confirmed on fresh ids 650-689 (f13 at D 5, 300 x D +.075; f13 at D 20-40, 30 x D +.095; f12-f14 at D 20-40, 100 x D +.045; f10-f12 at D 2-5, 30 x D +.047; all functions at D 5-10, 300 x D +.006).
- `c46`: `c45` with `prefix_low_beta=0.15` from D 10: in the ES prefix at 100 x D, a finite rugged curvature reading below 0.15 (Katsuura, f23) keeps the (1+1)-CMA-ES instead of the lq2 populations; confirmed on fresh ids 690-729 (f23 at D 10-40, 100 x D +.046); the best composite and the configuration frozen for the paper (2026-10-22).

## Provenance in the source

From 0.7.0 the source's comments and docstrings no longer cite research cycles, batches or the intermediate composites (`tests/test_no_research_ids.py` checks it); the measurements that justify a threshold stay next to it, and this page keeps which composite introduced which change. The research options that no shipped configuration used are gone (see `CHANGELOG.md`, 0.7.0); every preset above still loads and runs exactly as recorded.
